Streamer Live Test

1. About The Test

Before starting the test, please review the requirements and examples of good LIVE streams.

During the test you need to:

  • Open your trading platform
  • Act like you are doing a real TikTok LIVE
  • Interact with viewers
  • Explain your decisions
  • Complete trading scenarios

The goal of this test is to understand how well you can host a real LIVE stream.

2. Camera & Video Quality

Good example

GOOD CAMERA EXAMPLE
  • Face is clearly visible
  • Camera is stable
  • Good lighting
  • Comfortable viewing angle

Bad example

BAD CAMERA EXAMPLE
  • Face is too far away
  • Poor lighting
  • Unstable camera
  • Bad angle

3. Audio Quality

Good example

GOOD AUDIO EXAMPLE
  • Voice is clear
  • No strong background noise
  • Easy for viewers to understand

Bad example

BAD AUDIO EXAMPLE
  • Too much noise
  • Voice is difficult to hear
  • Bad sound quality

4. Communication With Audience

GOOD STREAM VIDEO EXAMPLE

A good streamer:

5. Test Rules

  • Camera must be ON
  • Record your screen during the whole test
  • Open your trading platform
  • Open the test chat next to it
  • Act like this is a real LIVE stream

Test duration: 5 minutes
